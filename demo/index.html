Photosensitivity warning

The flames jump with the music's beats, this page's own track included: every kick throws them up and down along the whole tube, and the light they cast on the backdrop brightens and darkens with them. Flashing light can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy. If you, or anyone who can see your screen, may be affected, choose Silence in the host row under the picture, or do not play the page.

The figures measured from the plugin's own output, against the broadcast and web line (Ofcom's, and WCAG's: three flashes in any second), are in the plugin's user guide. Pause, under the picture, stops it at once.
