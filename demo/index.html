Photosensitivity warning

The flames jump on the music's beats, and at the plugin's defaults they can flash: with this page's own track, or with loud music, more than three times a second in part of the picture. Flashing light can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy. If you, or anyone who can see your screen, may be affected, choose the Gas 30 Pa or Volume 130 dB preset, or Silence in the host row under the picture.

Broadcast and web guidance (Ofcom's, and WCAG's) puts the line at three flashes in any second, six rises and falls in brightness. Measured from the plugin's own output with xenon's rule (the whole frame and each third of it, at 60 fps): on this page's track at the defaults the flames made 8 rises and falls in their worst second in a third of the picture, over the line (2 over the whole picture); the Gas 30 Pa and Volume 130 dB presets made none. Through Resolume's demo clips at the defaults, its raw music stems mostly stayed within the line (2 of 33 clips over through the Over, at 7 and 8), but mastered music went over in a third of the picture (24 of 33 clips through the Over at about −11 dBFS RMS; all 33, up to 12, with louder masters); either preset brought every clip back under. The whole picture never went over at the defaults.

The tables are in the user guide's Flashing, and keeping it safe. The page opens on the plugin's defaults and changes none of them. Pause, under the picture, stops it at once.
